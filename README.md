# 🥖 Bread Stick Mod

## Description

Transform your Minecraft kitchen! **Bread Stick** brings a delicious variety of bread and breadstick items to enhance your culinary adventures. Create, craft, and enjoy new baking possibilities in your survival world.

## Features

🥖 **New Bread & Breadstick Varieties** - Expand your food collection with unique bread items and tasty breadstick variants

🔧 **Creative Crafting Recipes** - Discover new crafting recipes to prepare your favorite baked goods

🍽️ **Survival-Friendly** - Perfect for both casual players and hardcore survivors looking for fresh food options

## Compatibility

- **Minecraft Version:** 1.20.1 (with future versions planned)
- **Modloader:** Forge
- **Multiplayer Ready:** Works great on servers!

## Future Updates

More versions are coming! Stay tuned for 1.21.x support and potential new features.
